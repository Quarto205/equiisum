BioTech Innovations Ltd announced positive Phase 3 clinical trial results for its lead oncology compound BTX-401.
The randomized trial enrolled 650 patients across 42 clinical trial centers in North America and Europe.
Patients receiving BTX-401 demonstrated a median overall survival of 18.4 months compared to 12.1 months in the control group receiving standard chemotherapy.
Adverse events of grade 3 or higher were documented in 14% of treated patients versus 22% in the control arm.
Chief Medical Officer Dr. Eleanor Vance stated the primary endpoint of progression-free survival met statutory statistical significance with a p-value of less than 0.001.
Regulatory filings for accelerated approval are scheduled for submission to the United States Food and Drug Administration in the fourth quarter.
Global commercialization rights outside North America remain partnered with PharmaCorp International under a 2024 co-development agreement.
The company ended the period with cash equivalents of $185 million, sufficient to fund commercial manufacturing through 2028.
