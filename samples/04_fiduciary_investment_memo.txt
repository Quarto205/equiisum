INVESTMENT COMMITTEE MEMORANDUM - FIDUCIARY AUDIT REVIEW
Target Asset: Horizon Logistics Infrastructure Fund IV ("Horizon IV")
Target Fund Size: $750 million with a hard cap of $850 million.
Recommended Commitment: $50 million from the Core Real Assets Portfolio.
Target Gross IRR: 14.5% to 16.0% with a net projected multiple of invested capital of 1.85x.
Current Gross Asset Value of existing portfolio seed assets is $320 million across six distribution hubs.
Total debt leverage is restricted to a maximum loan-to-value ratio of 55% at the fund level.
Management fees are set at 1.25% on committed capital during the four-year investment period and 1.00% on net invested capital thereafter.
Carried interest is structured as an 8.0% preferred return hurdle followed by a 20.0% carry with a standard 100% European waterfall catch-up.
The anchor sponsor has committed $25 million of general partner co-investment, representing 3.3% of target commitments.
Downside stress-testing indicates debt service coverage remains above 1.40x under a 200 basis point interest rate expansion scenario.
Fiduciary committee approval requires unconditional confirmation of all debt covenant constraints, fee structures, and capital allocation percentages before closing.
